Where the others are ahead

  • iPhone. Cubench is Android only. On iPhone, look at Acubemy or CubeSensei.
  • Acubemy: matches against other cubers, tournaments, a daily scramble, benchmarks against millions of solves, CMLL and ZBLL, and 2x2 smart cubes 2 3.
  • Cubeast: a query language over your solves, shareable solves and Stackmat support 6.
  • CubeSensei: per-case charts and a training planner, free 10.
  • GAN CubeStation: a large community, ranked battles and monthly competitions 7.
  • Kruxmind: free 1v1 battles and a blind memo trainer 11.
  • More cube brands: Acubemy also reads GoCube, Rubik's and Giiker cubes, and Cubeast GoCube and Giiker 1 6. Cubench reads GAN, MoYu and QiYi.

Where Cubench stands out

  • Everything it measures is free: reconstruction, per-case stats and drills, with no account and no ads.
  • Drills checked on the cube that catch the wrong angle, a rotation, or another algorithm.
  • Blind solves read back with buffers and targets, and LBL solves read step by step.
  • Your solves stay on your phone, and the code is open.
