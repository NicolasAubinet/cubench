Cubench on iPhone and PC?

Cubench is on Android today. Another platform is being considered, most likely a web version first: it would run on iPhone, Mac and PC with nothing to install. Tell us what you would use, and we'll email you if it comes.

It is not a promise yet. How many people sign up, and for which platform, will decide what gets built.

We'll email you only when Cubench comes to a platform you picked, or earlier with a beta to test if you ticked that box. You first get one email to confirm your address, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Details in the privacy policy.

Smart cubes in a browser

A browser can talk to a smart cube over Bluetooth in Chrome and Edge, on Windows, Mac, Linux and Android. Safari can't, on iPhone or on Mac: on an iPhone it would take a free browser such as Bluefy.
