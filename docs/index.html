Solve on your smart cube and Cubench writes the whole solve down: every step timed, every case named, every algorithm you used.

  • CFOP, Roux, LBL and blind. Blind solves come back with buffers and targets.
  • Recognition and execution timed apart, for every step.

Your cases, measured

  • Known, needs review, to learn: read from your solves.

FREE BETA

Want a plan? Try the coach.

The free stats show where you stand. The coach tells you what to do next, and whether it worked. For CFOP solvers, for now.

  • A plan every week, read from your own solves: up to three things to work on, each with its drill. Every point cites a figure you can check in the app.
  • Did it work? Each plan checks last week's work against your new solves.
  • Practice any time: your costliest OLLs and PLLs as one drill. Each case drops out once it hits its target time.
  • What to learn next: the OLLs and PLLs that pay off soonest, easy ones first, or your own pick.
  • F2L: optimize your algorithms, rotations and look-ahead.
  • A custom daily drill for the cases that slip, before they cost you time.
